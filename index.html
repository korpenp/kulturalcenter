Our Heritage

A culture shaped by land and sky

The Sámi people have inhabited the Eanodat region, Enontekiö in Finnish, for thousands of years. Their lifeways, language, and spiritual relationship with the fell landscape represent a living tradition of remarkable depth, carried through generations by knowledge, story, and craftsmanship.

Kasivarsi Saami Cultural Center exists not as a museum, but as a living cultural space birthed by Juha Tornensis — a place where traditions breathe, where knowledge is shared, and where the Sámi voice speaks for itself. We stand at Kilpisjärvi, the same wilderness our ancestors stood in for thousands of years.

Through authentic structures, intimate programs, and a deep respect for Sámi knowledge, we invite you into a world shaped by reindeer, seasons, duodji, and sky.

Authentic Structures

Korvakota / Goahti
Traditional Sámi Dwelling

An authentic turf-and-timber home used by generations of Sámi families. Experience the intimate warmth of a fire at the hearth, surrounded by centuries of living tradition.

03
Laavu
Open Wilderness Shelter

Open to the elements and the sky, the laavu is the setting for fire-cooked meals, oral storytelling, and deep encounter with the Arctic landscape in its rawest form.

04
Suonjir
Elevated Storage Rack

A traditional raised rack demonstrating the Sámi art of preservation and survival in the subarctic. A testament to ingenuity shaped entirely by the land itself.

05
Big Korvakota
Community Gathering Space · Already on Site

Our largest gathering kota already standing at Kilpisjärvi. A spacious space for group programs, cultural events, and community occasions. The heart of the grounds.

Getting Here

Your Journey to 69°N

We are located in Kilpisjärvi, directly in front of Cahkal Hotels, on road E8. Kilpisjärvi is remote — and that is precisely what makes it extraordinary. The journey to the far North is part of the experience.

E8 — The Only Road

Road E8 is the sole route into Kilpisjärvi. In winter, conditions can be demanding. Check road conditions via Fintraffic before you travel.
