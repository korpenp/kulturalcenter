Our Heritage

Living culture
of the North

Nestled in the remote wilderness of Kilpisjärvi, Käsivarsi Cultural Center brings the traditions of the Sámi people to life. The Enontekiö region — known in Sámi as Eanodat — has been home to the Sámi for thousands of years, and this center exists to honour and share that legacy.

Through authentic structures, immersive guided experiences, and a deep respect for Sámi knowledge, we invite visitors to step beyond the ordinary and into a world shaped by reindeer, seasons, and sky.

The Structures

Structures 2 – 5 Ready
Structure 02
Korvakota
Goahti
Traditional Sámi Dwelling

An authentic turf-and-timber home used by generations of Sámi families. Experience the intimate warmth of a fire in the hearth, surrounded by centuries of living tradition.

Structure 03
Laavu
Open Wilderness Shelter

Open to the elements and the sky, the laavu is the ideal setting for fire-cooked meals, storytelling under the stars, and nature-based programs in the Arctic landscape.

Structure 04
Suonjir
Elevated Storage Rack

A traditional raised rack that demonstrates the Sámi art of food preservation and survival in the subarctic. A testament to ingenuity shaped by the land itself.

Structure 05
Big Korvakota
Group Venue · Already on Site

Our largest gathering space — already standing at Kilpisjärvi. A spacious kota for group events, cultural programs, and guided experiences. Capacity for larger parties and private bookings.
